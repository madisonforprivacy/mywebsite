What are Automatic License Plate Readers (ALPRs)?

 

Automated License Plate Readers (ALPRs) are cameras that capture a series of high resolution images every time a vehicle drives by them. These images are analyzed with AI software to create a digital fingerprint of each vehicle, allowing them to be searched not just by their license plate number but by the make, model, color, and any unique features like bumper stickers or roof racks.

Over time, this data is used to create a searchable record of every vehicle’s movement patterns. This record does not just stay within our local Madison County Sheriff Department, it is shared across a nationwide database that thousands of agencies have access to.   

These cameras collect data on all of our of vehicles regardless of whether the driver is suspected of a crime. These systems are marketed as indispensable tools to fight crime, but they ignore the powerful tools police already have to track criminals, such as cell phone location data, creating a loophole that doesn't require a warrant.

Why Should You Be Concerned

ALPRs invade your privacy and violate your civil liberties. Here's how:

ALPRs Are a Major Security Risk

Modern ALPR systems collect and store license plate data from every passing vehicle, creating huge databases of sensitive information that can be misused if breached or abused.

  • EFF: ALPR Surveillance Explained
  • ACLU: License Plate Readers and Privacy
ALPRs Do Not Reduce Crime

Studies show that ALPRs have little effect on reducing crime. They primarily gather license plate data without addressing underlying causes.

  • Brookings: ALPR and Public Safety
ALPRs Are Dragnet Surveillance

ALPRs track every passing vehicle, creating a massive database of movements without suspicion of wrongdoing.

  • ACLU: Dragnet ALPR Surveillance
ALPRs Are Abused

Without strict oversight, law enforcement can misuse ALPR data for unrelated investigations or personal tracking.

  • EFF: ALPR Abuse Examples
ALPR Usage Leads to Dangerous Police Encounters

Tracking vehicles can escalate routine stops into dangerous encounters, especially if misidentifications occur.

  • Cato Institute: ALPR and Police Risk
ALPRs Result in Racist Policing

ALPRs can disproportionately target marginalized communities, reinforcing systemic biases in law enforcement.

  • NAACP: ALPR and Racial Bias
ALPRs Put Undocumented People at Risk

ALPRs can expose undocumented people to immigration enforcement, increasing fear and risk in everyday life.

  • ACLU: Technology Risks for Immigrants
